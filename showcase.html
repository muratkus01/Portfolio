Linear Program Formulation & Storage Dynamics

The prosumer energy management system schedules residential flexible storage to minimize total net electricity procurement costs over a rolling prediction horizon H = 96 intervals (24 hours at 15-minute resolution):

min Σt=1H [ λtspot · Ptgrid,imp - λtfeed-in · Ptgrid,exp + cdeg · (Ptch + Ptdis) ] Δt

Subject to non-ideal electrochemical storage dynamics with round-trip efficiency η = 92% (charging ηch = 0.96, discharging ηdis = 0.96) and cyclic battery life degradation weighting cdeg = 0.015 EUR/kWh:

SoCt+1 = SoCt + [ ηch · Ptch - Ptdis / ηdis ] · (Δt / Enom) 0.10 ≤ SoCt ≤ 0.90, 0 ≤ Ptch ≤ 5.63 kW, 0 ≤ Ptdis ≤ 5.63 kW

Inverter conversion bounds enforce instantaneous power balance: Ptgrid,imp - Ptgrid,exp + Ptpv + Ptdis - Ptch = Ptload.

German Regulatory Framework: §14a & §41a EnWG

Under statutory German energy law (§41a EnWG), large electricity suppliers must offer dynamic spot retail tariffs linked directly to the EPEX SPOT Day-Ahead hourly/quarter-hourly clearing price. This exposes residential consumers to wholesale price volatility, creating economic incentives for automated price arbitrage.

Under §14a EnWG (effective January 2024), Distribution System Operators (DSOs) possess the legal right to dynamically throttle controllable consumer devices (Steuerbare Verbrauchseinrichtungen - SteuVE) including home batteries and heat pumps down to a 4.2 kW safety ceiling during grid congestion, while granting fixed or volumetric network charge reductions (Module 1-3).

Production Invariants & Edge Cases Caught

1. End-of-Horizon Hoarding Defect: Early receding-horizon formulations applied a linear terminal valuation to stored energy at the horizon boundary (t=96). Because the linear valuation matched the maximum retail import price, the solver aggressively imported high-priced grid power right before the horizon boundary to "hoard" artificial value, causing MPC to lose to simple heuristic rules. Removing linear terminal valuation unlocked unconstrained arbitrage.

2. Continuous Action Saturation Clipping: Initial Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) reinforcement learning policies exhibited an 80% saturation rate against the [-1, 1] output bound. Rescaling policy action bounds to physical battery converter kW limits dropped action clipping to 0.0%.

Hydraulic Head Physics & Binary Mutual Exclusion

The pumped storage multi-market controller maximizes net trading profits across Day-Ahead spot arbitrage, secondary frequency reserve (aFRR) capacity reservation, and expected balancing activations:

max Σt [ λtDA · (Ptturb - Ptpump) + λmcap · CmaFRR + λtact · AtaFRR - cwear · Wt ] Δt

Subject to non-linear upper reservoir volume dynamics (50,000 to 500,000 m3) and penstock head loss ΔH(q):

Vt+1 = Vt + (qtpump - qtturb) Δt, utturb + utpump ≤ 1, u ∈ {0, 1}

Machine modes must strictly enforce binary mutual exclusion to prevent catastrophic simultaneous hydraulic operations on reversible pump-turbines.

§118(6) EnWG Network Fee Exemption & Balancing Rules

Under German energy statute §118(6) EnWG, pumped storage power plants commissioned prior to 2026 are exempt from paying local network grid usage charges (Netzentgelte) on pumped electrical energy for 20 years. This statutory exemption provides a 30-50 EUR/MWh operational margin advantage.

Balancing reserve qualification requires committing 4-hour symmetric or asymmetric capacity blocks to transmission system operators (TSOs), reserving dynamic headroom for automated frequency restoration.

Bugs Caught by Production Physics Checks

1. Fractional Ramp Bypassing: Relaxing machine commitment variables to continuous intervals allowed an LP solver to pump at 30% and turbine at 70% simultaneously. This cancelled net hydraulic flow to simulate zero ramping, circumventing the 15 MW/min physical ramp rate limit and illegitimately beating the B2 ceiling. Binary integer enforcement ut + up ≤ 1 restored physical reality across all 12 scenario tests.

2. Balancing Flutter Wear Explosion: Symmetric capacity bidding subjected the plant to rapid alternating activation calls, causing 48 rotor reversals in 10 days (72,000 EUR in wear). Enforcing action smoothing (α = 0.5) and unidirectional capacity bidding slashed wear by 98% (1 reversal, 1,200 EUR wear).

The charging management system schedules multi-vehicle fleet charging behind a grid interconnection constraint to minimize total electricity cost while ensuring departure energy guarantees:

min Σt Σi ∈ Vt [ λtspot · pi,t Δt ] + Σi βi (Eireq - Eifinal)2

Subject to dynamic feeder capacity and vehicle battery energy continuity:

Σi ∈ Vt pi,t ≤ Pdim(t), 0 ≤ pi,t ≤ min(Piev,max, Pjplug,max), ei,t+1 = ei,t + ηi pi,t Δt

Where Pdim(t) represents the dynamic physical grid capacity envelope mandated by the distribution grid operator.

§14a EnWG Dynamic Dimming & Network Tariffs

Under §14a EnWG, Distribution System Operators (DSOs) can order temporary power reductions (dimming) on private and commercial charging hubs during grid bottlenecks. Rather than outright disconnection, the law mandates a minimum controllable capacity allowance.

Operators choosing dynamic dimming receive statutory network tariff discounts under Module 1 (flat reduction) or Module 2/3 (percentage and time-variable grid fee discounts).

Production Invariants & Departure Safety Floor

Collective Peak Contention Defect: Standard spot-minimizing LP formulations scheduled all connected vehicles to charge simultaneously during the cheapest spot price hour. Although individual connector constraints were respected, total concurrent power demand breached the site transformer limit, resulting in 108 vehicle departure shortfalls.

Earliest Deadline First (EDF) Reservation Layer: Implementing an aggregate cumulative energy reservation floor ordered by departure urgency eliminated all shortfalls, guaranteeing 0 missed departures under all dimming levels.

Charging Policy Progression

• B0 Uncontrolled: Immediate maximum charging upon arrival (Subject to site capacity clipping).

• B1 Equal-Share Rule: Splits available site capacity equally among all currently plugged vehicles (473.07 EUR fleet cost).

• B3 Price-Aware Greedy with EDF: Optimizes charging times against spot price curves while enforcing the aggregate EDF safety floor (475.19 EUR, 0 missed departures).

Cooperative Game Formulation & Owen Core

The renewable energy community model evaluates cooperative surplus across all subsets of 20 heterogeneous prosumers and consumers (100 kW total PV):

v(S) = Σt=1T [ Cstandalone(S, t) - Ccooperative(S, t) - cnet · Eshared(S, t) ]

Where shared energy is defined by instantaneous simultaneous matching behind the local distribution feeder:

Eshared(S, t) = min( Σi ∈ S Pi,tgen, Σi ∈ S Pi,tload ) Δt

The core of the cooperative game is non-empty if and only if there exists an allocation vector x such that Σi ∈ N xi = v(N) and Σi ∈ S xi ≥ v(S) for all 1,023 non-empty sub-coalitions S ⊆ N.

EU RED II Art. 22 & German §42b EnWG

The European Union Renewable Energy Directive (RED II Article 22) mandates Member States to establish enabling frameworks for Renewable Energy Communities (RECs), ensuring participants can share locally generated renewable electricity without losing consumer rights.

Under German energy law §42b EnWG (Gemeinschaftliche Gebaeudeversorgung), local energy sharing exempts intra-community flows from wholesale supplier margin overheads, but remains subject to local low-voltage network tariffs (cnet) and metering operator fees.

The 0.110 EUR/kWh Break-Even Cliff

Characteristic Function Valuation Defect: Early implementations mixed retail import savings with operator feed-in remuneration inside the sub-coalition value function, corrupting individual rationality constraints.

Core Collapse Boundary: An exact combinatorial sweep of all 210 - 1 = 1,023 sub-coalitions revealed that community cooperation is strictly sustainable up to cnet = 0.110 EUR/kWh. Beyond this fee, no cost-allocation mechanism (Shapley, Nucleolus, or Proportional) can prevent coalitions from defecting back to standalone retail contracts.

Mechanism Stability Comparison

• Standalone Baseline: Individual uncoordinated retail contracts (0 EUR community surplus).

• Shapley Value: Averages marginal contributions across all coalition permutations (Unstable: 443 blocking sub-coalitions defect).

• Centralized Optimization: Global cost minimization LP (Unstable: 2 singletons leave).

• Owen Core Allocation: Coalition-structured game-theoretic division guaranteeing stability across all 1,023 sub-coalitions (100% Structurally Stable, 0 blocking coalitions).

Over-Planting MILP & Point of Common Coupling (POC) Constraints

The co-located hybrid plant controller maximizes total wholesale market revenue from 50 MW wind and 30 MW solar behind a strict 40 MW grid connection limit, accounting for battery cell degradation:

max Σt [ λtDA · Ptgrid - cdeg · (Ptbat,chg + Ptbat,dis) ] Δt

Subject to the invariant point of common coupling capacity boundary and battery dynamics:

0 ≤ Ptgrid ≤ PPOCmax = 40.0 MW, Ptgrid = Ptwind,inj + Ptpv,inj + Ptbat,dis
Et+1 = Et + [ ηchg Ptbat,chg - (1 / ηdis) Ptbat,dis ] Δt, SOCmin ≤ Et / Enom ≤ SOCmax

Where total harvestable renewable generation (up to 80 MW) is dynamically partitioned between direct grid export, battery storage charging, and controlled physical curtailment.

EEG §51 / §51a Statutory Negative Spot Remuneration Suspensions

Under §51 of the German Renewable Energy Sources Act (EEG), market premium payments and statutory feed-in remuneration are entirely suspended when Day-Ahead spot prices remain negative for consecutive hours (progressing from historical 4-hour windows to single-hour suspensions in recent legal revisions).

Injecting renewable energy into negative spot prices without subsidy yields immediate commercial loss. The hybrid plant controller treats negative pricing as an active economic signal: clean energy that would otherwise be curtailed is diverted into the battery, stored, and injected during peak evening hours when remunerations return to positive territory.

Curtailment Spillage Bug Autopsy & Negative Price Gating

Curtailment Spillage Defect: Early rolling-horizon MPC implementations computed a planned curtailment percentage from forecast weather and applied that fraction blindly to real-time generation. When actual generation was slightly below forecast, this logic needlessly spilled clean electricity even when the 40 MW POC interconnection had available headroom.

Invariant Correction: Reformulating dispatch in absolute power units and voluntarily restricting curtailment decisions strictly to statutory negative price intervals lifted B3 MPC performance from +3.8% to +5.4% over baseline, capturing 10,280 EUR in additional revenue without a single over-injection violation.

Operating Controller Progression

• B0 Standalone (No Battery): Wind and solar operate unbuffered; power exceeding 40 MW is curtailed (695.3 MWh spilled, 190,105 EUR revenue).

• B1 Heuristic Curtailment Avoidance: Rule-based controller charges the battery whenever total generation exceeds 40 MW and discharges during peak hours (199,059 EUR, +4.7% lift).

• B3 Rolling-Horizon MPC (12h Look-Ahead): Optimizes 48 quarter-hour steps forward under forecast uncertainty, scheduling battery storage cycles around price peaks and negative intervals (200,385 EUR, +5.4% lift, 98.9% of B2 ceiling).

• B2 Perfect Foresight MILP: Omniscient upper bound with perfect knowledge of weather and prices (200,561 EUR, +5.5% ceiling).

Pinball Quantile Loss & Asymmetric Cashout Optimization

Probabilistic meteorological forecasts optimize the asymmetric pinball loss across 9 empirical quantiles (τ ∈ {0.1, 0.2, ..., 0.9}):

Lτ(y, qτ) = max( τ · (y - qτ), (1 - τ) · (qτ - y) )
